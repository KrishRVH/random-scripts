Questions asked on Stack Overflow, 2010 – 2026

Monthly count of new questions, Jan 2010 through Aug 2026. Hover for values. The log scale shows what the linear view hides: the collapse after Nov 2022 is a straight line, i.e. a constant ~70%/yr decay.

Notes.
  1. Source: Stack Exchange API (/questions, filter=total), one call per calendar month, fetched 25 Aug 2026. Counts exclude questions that have since been deleted, so older months are slightly understated and the most recent months will shrink further as moderation removes posts.
  2. Aug 2026 is a partial month (25 of 31 days). Its dot is hollow; a full-month pace would be roughly 1,450.
  3. Decay rates are compound annual rates between the labelled months, not fitted trends.
